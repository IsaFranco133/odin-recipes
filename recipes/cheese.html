Bee's homemade cheese

Description

Don't have any cheese for dinner? Need something yummy to eat but you have no time and even less money and a single cup of yoghurt? Bee has your back! Sit back, relax, and watch how a professional handles it.

Ingredients

  • A cup of yoghurt (120gr aprox)

Steps

  1. Open the cup of yoghurt.
  2. Lick the lid.
  3. Put yoghurt on mouth (do NOT swallow yet!!!).
  4. Keep yoghurt in your mouth and gargle on it for about three hours.
  5. Wait until it adopts a more consistent texture.
  6. Once it does, you can swallow!
